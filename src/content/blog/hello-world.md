I keep notes while reading — half-formed connections, methods that took three
tries to get working, papers I want to come back to. Most of it stays in a
plain text file. This site is where the parts that survive contact with a
second draft go.

Expect two kinds of writing here:

- **Research notes.** Working through a method, a dataset, or a paper that
  changed how I think about something.
- **Essays.** Longer pieces where the argument matters more than the result.

There is no schedule and no comments section. If something here is wrong, I
would rather hear about it than not.

The [style showcase](../style-showcase/) post demonstrates how everything
renders.
